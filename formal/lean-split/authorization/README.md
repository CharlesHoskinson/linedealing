# split-authorization oracle (Lean 4)

`SplitAuthorization/Spec.lean` is the executable reference semantics of the
OpenSpec capability `split-authorization`
(`openspec/changes/add-split-line-dealing/specs/split-authorization/spec.md`,
AU-001 to AU-028). The state is the FenrirAssets contract (charters, asset
records with their authorizations, the used nullifiers, the hold accumulator,
the recovery transition) together with one committee node's board-first view
(the pending set, the in-force board-first calls and the nullifiers of landed
`bf_root` leaves).

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `SplitAuthorization/Spec.lean` | `St`, `init`, `Op`, `Obs`, `Refusal`, the statement (`sourceOk`, `checksOk`, `slotOk`, `statementOk`), settlement (`settleIn`, `afterCarry`), `authorizeRefusals`, `revokeRefusals`, `bfChecks`, `inForceMidnight`, `inForceBoard`, `opRefusals`, `exec`, `apply`, `runOps`, `validState`, `stepRefusals` |
| `SplitAuthorization/Theorems.lean` | the invariants and claims C6 and C11 over every run from `init` |
| `SplitAuthorization/Tests.lean` | pins from the scenarios (`decide +kernel` and `#guard`), the AU-029 scenario (ledger 11) |
| `SplitAuthorization/Audit.lean` | `#print axioms` of every theorem and `#audit_ns SplitAuthorization`, pinned (generated by `../scripts/gen_audit.py`) |
| `Main.lean` | the codec only, through `../codec` |

Build and gates (from `formal/lean-split`, under `heavy-run`):

    (cd authorization && lake build --wfail)
    ./gates.sh oracle authorization
    ./gates.sh trace authorization

## Modes

Default: one `kink-implementation/1` request with `inputs = {"params": {"mode",
"q"}, "ops"}`. The answer is `{"end": "final", "cells": {"state", "obs"}}` with
one observation per operation. A refused operation is the observation
`{"refused": CODES}` and changes nothing; the run goes on.

`--step`: JSON Lines `{"state", "op"}` to `{"state", "obs"}`; a refused
operation gives `{"refused": CODES}`; a state outside `validState` gives
`{"refused": ["outside-state-domain"]}`; a line that does not decode gives
`{"error": TEXT}`.

A nullifier is `{"fresh": n}` (a record birth) or `{"auth": a, "outcome": O}`,
the successor nullifier `N(nsk, a, O)`.

Operations (`"op"`): `charter`, `record` (a birth lands), `authorize` (a call
reaches Midnight), `finalize` (its transaction is final at a height), `revoke`,
`settle_root` (leaves `[auth, outcome]`), `bf_root` (tuples), `recover`,
`bf_call` (the board orders a board-first call), `query` (is `auth` in force at
a height), `seizure` (admission of a seizure request: does every output pay
`D_reg`). Observations: `accepted`, `refused`, `ok`, `in_force`, `void` (the
reason).

## Claims

| Claim | Theorem | Statement |
|---|---|---|
| C11 (AU-024) | `C11_one_final` | in every run from `init`, a Midnight-first record holds at most one authorization that settled FINAL, over all the successors of its record states |
| C11 (AU-008) | `C11_nf_once` | in every run, the nullifiers of the accepted `authorize` calls are distinct |
| C11 (AU-025) | `C11_released_never_fresh` | in every run, a record with an authorization that settled RELEASED holds no fresh nullifier |
| C11 (AU-025, AU-029) | `C11_pinned_never_unspent` | in every run, a record that holds a pin (from a RELEASED settlement on, through every VOID re-post) is never UNSPENT |
| C11, C6 (AU-015, AU-016) | `C11_bf_first_only` | in every run, the node holds at most one in-force board-first call per `nf` |
| C6 (AU-010) | `C6_no_force_without_finalize` | an authorization that no `finalize` made final is in force at no Midnight height |

These are the state parts of the claims. Board-first records are outside
`C11_one_final` (ledger 13). Ledger 11 is ruled by AU-029 (DECISIONS 60): a
LOCKED record whose pinned re-post settles VOID stays LOCKED to the same pin, and
a later authorization on another input is refused with `pin_mismatch`
(`pin_au029_locked_void`).

## Abstractions

* Hashes are collision free and are inputs: `auth`, `rh`, `cm_addr`, the hold
  tag and the slot roots are numbers.
* A nullifier is fresh at birth or `N(nsk, auth, outcome)` for a settlement
  successor, so two successors never share a nullifier and none is fresh.
* The `authorize` proof is the statement it proves: the call carries the
  record, the nullifier, the source, the named slot and the outcomes of the
  checks that read data outside the contract (inputs and fee ceiling,
  predicates and approvers, `S ∈ Γ`, the registry blacklist, the ticket). The
  hold accumulator, the record fields and the recovery transition are read
  from the state.
* Checkpoint landings, carried leaves with their signature counts, Midnight
  finality heights and the recovery transition enter as operations.
* The node's board-first checks read the proof, the root's finality and age,
  the bound, the interval and `F_M` as inputs of the call.
