# split-shard-holders oracle (Lean 4)

`SplitShardHolders/Spec.lean` is the executable reference semantics of the
OpenSpec capability `split-shard-holders`
(`openspec/changes/add-split-line-dealing/specs/split-shard-holders/spec.md`,
SH-001 to SH-038). The state is the view of the actors and of FenrirAssets: the
fixed charters `Γ`, the BIRTH_OK signatures of the committee nodes, the admitted
address records with their `acked` flag, the actors' pools, the answered pool
pairs, the ECDSA slots an actor contributed on and the ACK_OK signatures of the
committee nodes (SH-030 to SH-037, DECISIONS 67).

| Module | Holds |
|---|---|
| `SplitShardHolders/Spec.lean` | `Gate`, `validCharter` (SH-001, SH-002), `adm`, `admitted` (SH-003), `weight` (SH-004), `clActors` (SH-038), `halves` (SH-014), `guardianSlots` (SH-020), `sessionKeep` (SH-016), `putEntries` (SH-017), `birthPasses` (SH-007 to SH-009), `ackPasses` (SH-031), `noAckCert` (SH-033), `payBlocked` (SH-035), `setAcked` (SH-037), `St`, `init`, `Op`, `Obs`, `Refusal`, `opRefusals`, `apply`, `runOps`, `validState`, `stepRefusals`, the theorems |
| `SplitShardHolders/Tests.lean` | pins from the scenarios (`decide +kernel` and `#guard`), witnesses |
| `SplitShardHolders/Audit.lean` | `#print axioms` of every theorem and `#audit_ns SplitShardHolders`, pinned (generated by `../scripts/gen_audit.py`) |
| `Main.lean` | the codec only, through `../codec` |

Build and gates (from `formal/lean-split`, under `heavy-run`):

    (cd shard-holders && lake build --wfail)
    ./gates.sh oracle shard-holders
    ./gates.sh trace shard-holders

## Modes

Default: one `kink-implementation/1` request with `inputs = {"params": {"n",
"f", "mode", "N_h"}, "ops"}`. Parameters outside RQ-001 (`f ≥ 1`,
`n ≥ 3f + 1`) give `{"end": "could-not-apply", "reason":
"outside-capability"}`. Otherwise the answer is `{"end": "final", "cells":
{"state", "obs"}}` with one observation per operation. A refused operation is
the observation `{"refused": CODES}` and changes nothing; the run goes on.

`--step`: JSON Lines `{"state", "op"}` to `{"state", "obs"}`; a refused
operation gives `{"refused": CODES}`; a state outside `validState` gives
`{"refused": ["outside-state-domain"]}`; a line that does not decode gives
`{"error": TEXT}`.

A gate is a JSON object: `{"gate": "LEAF", "mid", "actor"}`, `{"gate": "AND",
"mid", "children"}`, `{"gate": "GROUP", "mid", "k", "children"}` or
`{"gate": "OR", "mid", "primary", "escrow"}`. `mid` is the member id of the
gate in its group parent (0 elsewhere). The top gate `Fenrir AND Γ` is
implicit.

Operations (`"op"`): `fix_charter` (a charter id and its `Γ`), `birth_check`
(one node checks the BIRTH request of one `cm_addr`), `submit_record` (an
address record arrives at FenrirAssets), `post_pool` (an actor posts a pool of
64 pairs signed by a pool key), `contribute` (an actor answers a request),
`session` (the slots of one session and the check of each), `put_shares` (the
slots of one put, TEE mode), `holder_open` (the holder wallet first opens the
record of a `cm_addr`; `x_match` is `x_u·G = X_u`), `ack_check` (one node checks
the ACK envelope of one `cm_addr`), `acknowledge` (the holder's call at
FenrirAssets with the nodes of its ACK_OK certificate), `pay` (the payer wallet
reads the record of a `cm_addr` and would pay to the address). Observations:
`ok`, `birth_ok`, `refused`, `contributed` (`refill`: fewer than `N_h` unused
pairs remain), `session` (`kept`, `complained`), `puts` (the put entries),
`ack_envelope` (the holder wallet sends the ACK envelope), `ack_ok` (the node
signs ACK_OK), `paid` (the payer wallet pays).

The operations of DECISIONS 67, in JSON:

    {"op": "holder_open", "cm_addr": N, "x_match": B}
    {"op": "ack_check", "node": N, "cm_addr": N, "ack": {"x_recomputed": B, "opening_ok": B, "pop_u": B}}
    {"op": "acknowledge", "cm_addr": N, "signers": [N, ...]}
    {"op": "pay", "cm_addr": N}

A `submit_record` record carries `network_created` (ledger 14). A stored record
is `{"cm_addr", "profile", "charter", "sigs", "network_created", "acked"}`, and
the state has `ack_ok`, the `[node, cm_addr]` pairs of the ACK_OK signatures.

`no_birth_certificate`, `no_escrow_certificate`, `outside-capability`,

These are the state parts of the claims. C3, C4, C5, C18 and C19 and the
cryptographic parts of C1, C2 and C17 (unforgeability, the hiding of the
shards, the binding of the tweak) are properties of the signature scheme and
are outside the oracle (SH-027 to SH-029 are exceptions in `trace.json`).

## Abstractions

* Hashes and keys are numbers. A proof of possession is the context it was
  made for and a validity flag; a signature or an ACK verifies exactly when
  the input says so.
* The dealer proofs, the recomputed `X_γ` and `X`, the address encoding and the
  opening of `cm_addr` are flags of the birth check.
* The actor's own policy (SH-023) and its recomputation of the request (SH-021)
  are flags of the contribution.
* Of the commitment list `CL` (SH-038) the oracle holds the actors it names.
  Its pool pairs, slot and points, and the hash that gives `ρ_a`, are outside
  the state.
* The holder wallet's check `x_u·G = X_u` (SH-030, SH-036) is one flag of
  `holder_open`. The checks of an ACK envelope (SH-031) are three flags, and an
  ACK_OK signature verifies exactly when the node gave it in the run. The
  extraction of `x_u` from `pop_u` (C1, DECISIONS 67) is outside the oracle.
* Weights are exact fractions: the coefficient of member `x` in the used set
  `T` is `Π_{y ∈ T, y ≠ x} y / (y − x)` (ledger 10).
* Randomness (SH-005, SH-024), the tweak (SH-006, SH-025) and the signature
  scheme (SH-027 to SH-029) are outside the state.
