# Architecture: split-line dealing

Baseline BASE-001 revision 4, traceability TRACE-001 revision 4. Both files are generated by [tools/gen.py](tools/gen.py) from the capability specs of the OpenSpec change `add-split-line-dealing` and from the DECISIONS entries that the specs cite. [tools/check.py](tools/check.py) checks them against the specs and the records.

## Sources and authority

The design of record is `design/enhancement/DESIGN.md` at main 4a20ccc (SRC-001) with DECISIONS 1 to 64 (SRC-002). DECISIONS 59 to 64 fix the findings F1 to F6 of the spec-only Quint model (`formal/quint-spec/FINDINGS.md`). Where `impl/` and DESIGN disagree, DESIGN wins. `impl/` is evidence only (SRC-006), and its gaps are listed at the end of this note.

## Model depth

Domain modelling applies: the protocol has seven bounded contexts with their own vocabulary, one external context, seven aggregates with distinct consistency boundaries and eight cross-context contracts. Each context maps to one OpenSpec capability and one module.

| Context | Capability | IDs | Module | Aggregates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTX-001 request lifecycle | `split-request` | RQ-001 to RQ-074 | MOD-001 request state machine | AGG-001 request |
| CTX-002 slot pools | `split-slots` | SL-001 to SL-030 | MOD-002 slot manager | AGG-002 committee slot pool, AGG-003 ECDSA account pool |
| CTX-003 authorization ledger | `split-authorization` | AU-001 to AU-029 | MOD-003 FenrirAssets contract | AGG-004 asset record, AGG-005 authorization |
| CTX-004 checkpoint and restart | `split-checkpoint` | CP-001 to CP-039 | MOD-004 checkpoint writer | AGG-006 checkpoint chain |
| CTX-005 envelope | `split-envelope` | EN-001 to EN-023 | MOD-005 envelope codec | (value objects of AGG-001) |
| CTX-006 shard holders | `split-shard-holders` | SH-001 to SH-029 | MOD-006 actor wallet and birth coordinator | AGG-007 actor nonce pool |
| CTX-008 committee handoff | `split-handoff` | HO-001 to HO-068 | MOD-007 handoff state machine | (state of the committee epochs; no new aggregate) |
| CTX-007 seald Sessions | external (Veritee SS-001 to SS-145) | | | |

The envelope context owns no aggregate. An envelope is immutable after its post, and its identity `eid` is the identity of the request it carries.

## Contracts between contexts

- CON-001 board order and slot precedence (CTX-002 to CTX-001): slots are named, reserved, claimed and burned by board position. Q-003 records the wait for earlier headers.
- CON-002 authorization read (CTX-003 to CTX-001): release needs an authorization in force at a Midnight finality depth, its revocation height and, in P2, the hold wait `Δ_hold`.
- CON-003 checkpoint contract (CTX-004 to CTX-003): one Midnight write per checkpoint carries the pool fields, seat roots, `settle_root` and `bf_root`. No committee write per request (DECISIONS 52, owner ruling).
- CON-004 seald Sessions slot API (CTX-007 to CTX-002 and CTX-004): `slot_bind`, `slot_put` and restore under the peer witness (DECISIONS 51).
- CON-005 envelope frame (CTX-005 to CTX-001): one 16,384-byte frame, public checks before order, threshold-PRF keys after order.
- CON-006 contribution (CTX-006 to CTX-001): an actor fills one position, and the combiner checks the scalars at release.
- CON-007 birth certificate (CTX-006 to CTX-003): `q` BIRTH_OK signatures and escrow certificates admit a record.
- CON-008 activation record (CTX-008 to CTX-004 and CTX-001): one Midnight record signed by `t'` seats of the armed epoch carries `EKS_{e+1}`, the roster, the ROTATE and the heartbeat under `X_f`, and each live request has one owning epoch at every board position (DECISIONS 80, 83).

## Midnight writes

Every Midnight write that the specs allow is per checkpoint, per epoch, per boot or per user transaction. The committee writes nothing per request, per slot or per put. Items that need durability go to the seat logs on the board, and a root per checkpoint covers them.

| Writer | Write | Count |
|---|---|---|
| committee | checkpoint (pool fields, seat roots, `settle_root`, `bf_root`, restart marker) | 1 per checkpoint interval (CP-003, CP-005) |
| committee | activation record with `EKS_{e+1}`, roster, ROTATE and heartbeat, in place of one checkpoint | 0 extra, 1 per handoff (CP-006, HO-047) |
| node | boot ticket | 1 per boot |
| user or source | authorization (`authorize`), revocation, record birth | 1 per user action |
| actor | pool-key root | 1 per `N_pk = 1,024` pool keys (SH-013) |

## Decisions

The baseline has one D item for each DECISIONS entry that a requirement cites: entries 8, 19 to 22, 27 to 29, 34 to 36, 38, 40, 42 to 72 and 79 to 86. `check.py` derives that set from the specs again and fails if a D item is missing or extra. The D identity is the entry number (`D-042` is DECISIONS 42), so the change adds no second numbering. `record_ref` points to the entry. The repository's convention is one MADR entry per numbered item in DECISIONS.md, and entries 42 to 63 already follow it. This change adds indented MADR fields under the consumed entries 8 to 40 and leaves the entry text as it was.

State: entries on main are `confirmed`, because an owner ruling or a lead decision under the owner's delegation (LOOP.md) made them. D-053 and D-054 are `provisional` until quint-split merges (Q-004). DECISIONS 53 has no Drivers or Considered options field (Q-006).

Every check in `traceability.yaml` is `planned`. LOOP item 13 (Lean oracle) checks conformance of every obligation, LOOP item 14 (Quint model) checks the 19 claims that the specs cite (C1 to C13, C16 to C21) as invariants, and item 11 checks the adoption in `impl/` for D-051 to D-054 and D-057. The vectors and Quint runs that the DECISIONS confirmations name supported the choices before these specs existed. They are not checks of these specs.

## Questions

Q-001 reason codes MALFORMED, DEALER_FAULT, PIN_SPENT and RESTART; Q-002 the reason of a VOID tombstone (resolved in revision 3); Q-003 the wait for earlier headers in committee slot precedence; Q-004 the merge of quint-split; Q-005 the values of `B`, `B_slot` and `S_max`; Q-006 the missing fields of DECISIONS 53; Q-007 the SHALL form and code of the actor's MAY (SH-023); Q-008 the re-end of a request that ended after the latest landed checkpoint when a restart burns its slots (RQ-048, DECISIONS 53). Q-001 to Q-006 change a wire value, a symbol's number or a record's text, never an outcome.

The lead resolved two questions in revision 2:

- Q-008: RQ-068 ends again a request that ended after the latest landed checkpoint when a whole-committee restart burns one of its slots, and its outcome follows the restart settlement rule of RQ-052 (DECISIONS 53). RQ-005 excepts this re-end, and RQ-048 points to it. DECISIONS 58 settles the conflict with RQ-054: the restart leaf supersedes a settlement leaf that Midnight does not hold, and a leaf that Midnight holds (under a landed `settle_root` or carried by a reissue) is never replaced. Reason: DESIGN §5.6 and §7.11 say the restart ends such a request again, and without the rule claim C11 can break. RQ-068 maps to INV-011, D-053 and D-058, and RQ-054 to INV-011 and D-058.
- Q-007: SH-023 stays a SHALL refusal conditioned on the actor's own policy, with the policy a free model input and the code `policy_refused`. Reason: OpenSpec strict needs SHALL or MUST, and the condition keeps the "may refuse" meaning of DESIGN §4.2.

The lead resolved Q-002 in revision 3:

- Q-002: the reason of a VOID tombstone is the reason that at least `f + 1` of its counted posts carry, and MIXED when no reason reaches `f + 1` (RQ-045, RQ-072). No rule reads MIXED as STALLED, SLOT_TAKEN or REVOKED (RQ-071; RQ-063, AU-019, AU-021). Reason: with the reason of the completing post a corrupt member chose the reason (item 14 finding F2), against C13 and C20. DECISIONS 61, D-061.

## Exclusions

The cryptographic instantiation of `F_presig` and `F_mul` (O1, O2, O19), rotation (C14, O5), recovery (C15), the exit leaf, the ticket proof circuit (DECISIONS 30) and the seald store itself (SRC-007) are out of scope. The specs state the interface that each one gives.
