- Papers: cite a paper only after reading it in full from a local copy.
- Paper prose: load the `my-voice` skill before writing and run `voice-audit` as the final pass. Positions stated with conviction; no process history in the paper.
- Commit after each verified milestone, author Charles Hoskinson <charles.hoskinson@gmail.com>, no AI co-author trailer. Never push.
- Decide disagreements yourself with reasons; record them in the Log. Ask the owner only when blocked on a decision that is genuinely theirs.
