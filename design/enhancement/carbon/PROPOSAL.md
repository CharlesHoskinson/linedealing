# Blinded Co-Line Dealing: Two-Key Covenant Custody and Operator-Blind Execution for Fenrir

## 1. Summary

**The enhancement.** Blinded Co-Line Dealing (`BCLD`) extends [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md) from single-key owner custody on a plaintext bulletin board to two-key (and optional three-key) covenant co-custody over an operator-blind execution pipeline across Bitcoin, Ethereum (EVM), and Solana. The owner still acts **once, offline**, with **no interactive round per spend**, and normal spends emit **native single-key signatures** (`BIP-340` Schnorr on Bitcoin `P2TR`, `secp256k1` ECDSA on Ethereum EOAs and Bitcoin `P2WPKH`, `Ed25519` on Solana). Neither the user nor any coalition of up to all $n$ Fenrir nodes holds the controlling private key or can bypass the asset's charter.

**The one idea that makes it work.** [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L3-L6) observes that both Schnorr ($s(c) = k + c x$) and ECDSA ($s(h) = h w + r \mu$, with $w = k^{-1}, \mu = k^{-1} x$) are affine lines whose Feldman constant terms verify publicly on the signature curve. `BCLD` **splits the signature line additively between the offline principal and the threshold committee** while moving all identity, ownership, and covenant evaluation into a **Midnight Zero-Knowledge Authorization Certificate (`ZKAC`)**:

1. **Additive split of keys and lines.** Every Fenrir address has joint secret $x = x_U + (x_F + \text{tw}) \bmod q$, where $x_U$ stays exclusively in the user's wallet, $x_F$ is the Fenrir committee's single master DKG scalar (`t = 7` of `n = 9, f = 2` at launch; `t = 12` of `n = 17, f = 5` at scale), and $\text{tw} \in \mathbb{Z}_q$ is an unlinkable per-address tweak committed inside Midnight's shielded state. On a spend, the owner never deals $x_U$. Instead, across a fee grid $j = 1..K$ spending one pinned conflict object `pin`, the owner deals **sealed user half-signatures** `Share_t(s_{U,j})`:
   - **Schnorr (Suites 2 & 3):** $s_{U,j} = \sigma_j k_{U,j} + c_j x_U$, checked against $F_{0,U}^{(j)} = \sigma_j R_{U,j} + c_j X_U$. Committee node $i$ adds $s_{F,j^*,i} = \sigma_{j^*}(d_{F,i} + \rho_{j^*} e'_{F,i}) + c_{j^*}(x_{F,i} + \text{tw}_i)$ from an epoch nonce pool, with **zero setup interaction** at address creation.
   - **ECDSA (Suite 1):** Using a one-time 1-round `DKLs23` OT-OLE setup at address arming (`K = 1` on EIP-1559 Ethereum), user and committee hold additive presignature shares $w_j = w_{U,j} + w_{F,j}$ and $\mu_j = \mu_{U,j} + \mu_{F,j}$ satisfying $W_{U,j} + W_{F,j} = G$ and $M_{U,j} + M_{F,j} = X$ in base $R_j$. Offline, the owner deals $s_{U,j} = h_j w_{U,j} + r_j \mu_{U,j}$, checked against $F_{0,U}^{(j)} = h_j W_{U,j} + r_j M_{U,j}$, and node $i$ adds $s_{F,j^*,i} = h_{j^*} w_{F,j^*,i} + r_{j^*} \mu_{F,j^*,i}$.

   Summed constant terms yield $\sigma_{j^*} R_{j^*} + c_{j^*} X$ (Schnorr) and $h_{j^*} G + r_{j^*} X$ (ECDSA). Line dealing's sealed guarantee survives intact: even if **all $n$ committee nodes collude**, they never learn $x_U$ and cannot sign outside $\{tx_1, \dots, tx_K\}$; conversely, a thief with $x_U$ cannot move funds without Fenrir's share and a Midnight `ZKAC`. Committee rotation reshares $O(1)$ master scalars with **zero on-chain coin movement**.

2. **Reconciling R1, R4(b) seizure, and R5 exit.** We take the explicit position that **R1 means no valid spend occurs without a settled Midnight `ZKAC` proving one of the charter's three admitted authorization sources** — (a) principal intent with sealed user co-dealing, (b) charter-admitted legal/default seizure to an immutable charter sink `D_reg`, or (c) sweep to the owner's standing exit `D_exit` — while the committee never holds $x_U$. Normal spends are native single-key signatures; seizure and Fenrir-disappearance exit use pre-signed CPFP-anchored sweeps and Taproot `<T_exit> OP_CSV` leaves on Bitcoin, native `ERC-3643` / `Token-2022` hooks on regulated tokens, and minimal immutable 3-path vaults only where uncooperative native-coin seizure is enabled by the charter.

3. **Two layered modes with one wire format.** Every board submission is a fixed 16 KiB `OrderEnvelope` authenticated by a Midnight `Zswap` fee-burn proof and threshold-encrypted under BLS12-381 key $Y_{\text{mem}}$. Only after BFT quorum certificate `QC_order` fixes order do signing nodes exchange decryption shares over private channels.
   - **TEE mode (v1):** Each operator runs its Fenrir node, mempool, decryption, `Build`/`Select`, signing, and RAM zeroization inside an AMD SEV-SNP confidential VM built on [veritee-vm](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/README.md), extended with sealed storage, peer-quorum rollback leases, Midnight Warden-gated resharing across upgrades, and mutually attested fixed-padding `akx` channels. Threshold encryption and `BCLD` already run *inside* v1, so breaking $< t$ TEEs exposes nothing early and breaking **all $n$ TEEs** still cannot forge outside the user's sealed variants or steal $x_U$.
   - **Non-TEE mode (long-term):** Drops hardware trust with **zero wire-format, board, or key-model redesign**. Covenant predicates, owner identity, and deposit-to-egress links remain hidden inside Midnight's `ZKAC` (Plonky3 KoalaBear STARK $< 2^{18}$ rows wrapped once in Midnight's Halo2/BLS12-381 proof $< 2^{19}$ rows; committee runs **zero** proving circuits). In Blinded Sealed mode, even `(X, tx)` stays threshold-encrypted until the partial signatures combine for host broadcast.

## 2. Key and address model

### 2.1 Committee profiles and master keys

The committee has $n \ge 3f + 1$ nodes, Byzantine bound $f$, board quorum $q = \lceil(n + f + 1)/2\rceil$, and Shamir reconstruction threshold $t = n - f$ (profile T2 of [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L33-L40), promoted to default across all modes). Launch runs $(n = 9, f = 2, q = 6, t = 7)$; scale runs $(n = 17, f = 5, q = 12, t = 12)$. Profile T1 ($t = f + 1$, giving $t = 3$ at launch and $t = 6$ at scale) remains configurable per asset charter where liveness under simultaneous passive share loss outweighs $n - f$ collusion resistance, though `BCLD` enables coinless rotation at $t = n - f$ directly (§2.5).

At genesis, the committee executes a Pedersen-Feldman DKG over `[GS22b]` MEGa ciphertexts accompanied by §2.5's succinct Plonky3-in-BLS12-381 share-validity NIZK to establish five global master secrets — **never reconstructed in the clear**:

| Key | Group | Public key | Secret share at node $i$ | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Master co-signing (Suite 1/2) | `secp256k1` ($q$) | $X_F = x_F G$ | $[x_F]_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q$ | Joint address key $X = X_U + X_F + \text{tw}\cdot G$ on BTC & EVM |
| Master co-signing (Suite 3) | `edwards25519` ($\ell$) | $X_F^{\text{ed}} = x_F^{\text{ed}} B$ | $[x_F^{\text{ed}}]_i \in \mathbb{Z}_\ell$ | Joint address key on Solana |
| Charter enforcement / sweep (per curve) | `secp256k1` / `edwards25519` | $X_{\text{seize}} = x_{\text{seize}} G$ | $[x_{\text{seize}}]_i$ | Gated strictly by settled Midnight `SEIZE` or `SWEEP_EXIT` `ZKAC` |
| Sealed-mempool KEM | `BLS12-381` ($r$) | $Y_{\text{mem}} = y_{\text{mem}} G_1 \in \mathbb{G}_1$ | $[y_{\text{mem}}]_i \in \mathbb{Z}_r$ | Post-`QC_order` threshold decryption (`OrderEnvelope`) |
| Node Dealing KEM (per epoch $e$) | Suite curve | $\text{PK}_{e,i} = \text{sk}_{e,i} G$ | $\text{sk}_{e,i}$ | MEGa share encryption ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L46)) |

### 2.2 Who holds what and how an address is born

Every controlled address binds three algebraically independent elements (plus an optional fourth element when a third-party key is configured under R5):

1. **User share $x_U$ (held only by the user's wallet; committee holds nothing).** For every new deposit, vault, or change address $k$, the user's wallet samples a fresh scalar $x_{U,k} \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{Z}_q^*$ (or derives it via hardened BIP-32 / SLIP-0010 from a hardware-wallet master seed) and computes $X_{U,k} = x_{U,k} G$. A fresh $x_{U,k}$ is mandatory per new or change address (R3).
2. **Unlinkable network tweak $\text{tw}_k$ (committed in Midnight shielded state; hidden from chain observers).** The user samples a random 256-bit `tweak_seed_k`, derives $\text{tw}_k = Hq_{\text{BCLD/tw}}(\text{chain\_id} \parallel \text{charter\_hash} \parallel X_{U,k} \parallel \text{tweak\_seed}_k)$, and sets the Fenrir leg to $X_{F,k} = X_F + \text{tw}_k G$. No committee node can link $X_k$ to $X_F$ or to any other Fenrir address on-chain without opening `tweak_seed_k`.
3. **Optional third-party key $X_{A,k}$ (R5 three-key shape, when required by charter).** Named approvers (issuer, compliance officer, KYC provider) normally enforce vetoes at zero target-chain cost inside Midnight's `ZKAC` (§3.2). When an institutional charter explicitly demands cryptographic three-key custody on the signature curve, the third party supplies $X_{A,k} = x_{A,k} G$ and contributes a sealed partial $s_{A,j}$ identical in form to $s_{U,j}$.

The controlling joint public key is:
$$X_k = X_{U,k} + X_F + \text{tw}_k G \; (+ X_{A,k}) \quad \text{with implicit private key } x_k = x_{U,k} + x_F + \text{tw}_k \; (+ x_{A,k}) \bmod q.$$
For Suite 2 (`BIP-340`), if $X_k$ has odd $y$, both user and committee negate their scalars at spend time (parity normalization $\tau \in \{\pm 1\}$) before applying the BIP-341 Taproot tweak $t_{\text{tap}} = H_{\text{TapTweak}}(x(X_k) \parallel \text{merkle\_root})$. For Suite 3 (`Ed25519`), $x_{U,k}$ is derived as a full mod-$\ell$ scalar (`clamp(SHA-512(seed)[0..32]) mod ℓ`) rather than raw seed clamping so additive key aggregation preserves prime-order subgroup closure (`[ℓ]X_k = O`).

**Setup interaction per suite:**
- **Suites 2 & 3 (Bitcoin `P2TR`, Solana `Ed25519`) and cold/receive-only Suite 1 addresses:** **Zero interactive rounds with Fenrir.** The wallet computes $X_k$ locally from public $X_F$, registers the shielded `CovenantNote` commitment `cm_acct = Commit(asset_id, charter_hash, X_k, H(K_exit_U), D_exit, tweak_seed_k; r_acct)` on Midnight, and receives funds immediately.
- **Active Suite 1 (`secp256k1` ECDSA — Ethereum EOA, legacy Bitcoin `P2WPKH`):** **One 1-round `DKLs23` OT-OLE arming exchange** at address creation (or before first spend, over an anonymous `akx` / Tor session), establishing $K$ additive presignature splits ($K = 1$ for EIP-1559 Ethereum; $K = 8$ for legacy `P2WPKH`):
  $$R_j = (k_{U,j} k_{F,j}) G, \quad w_{U,j} + w_{F,j} = (k_{U,j} k_{F,j})^{-1} \bmod q, \quad \mu_{U,j} + \mu_{F,j} = (k_{U,j} k_{F,j})^{-1} x_k \bmod q.$$
  The user stores $(R_j, w_{U,j}, \mu_{U,j}, W_{U,j} = w_{U,j} R_j, M_{U,j} = \mu_{U,j} R_j)$; committee node $i$ stores Shamir shares $([w_{F,j}]_i, [\mu_{F,j}]_i)$ verified against $W_{F,j} = G - W_{U,j}$ and $M_{F,j} = X_k - M_{U,j}$ in base $R_j$.

### 2.3 What the target chain sees (and explicit justification of every script or contract path)

Normal owner-initiated and omnibus-egress spends produce **single native signatures** on all three chains. Where an asset charter mandates uncooperative seizure (`seizable = true`, R4(b)) or unilateral disappearance recovery (`T_exit`, R5), pure discrete-log hardness forbids a bare single key $X_k$ with no auxiliary path from simultaneously denying all-$n$-node theft (R1) and permitting movement when one side vanishes. `BCLD` resolves this per chain with minimal footprint:

| Target chain & asset class | On-chain representation | What normal spends put on chain | How R4(b) seizure & R5 disappearance exit are enforced on-chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bitcoin (`P2TR`, BIP-341)** — all charters | Native Taproot output `Q = TapTweak(X_k, root)` | **1 native 64-B `BIP-340` key-path signature** (script tree stays hidden) | **Leaf 1 (R5 exit, always present):** `<T_exit> OP_CSV OP_DROP <K_exit_U> OP_CHECKSIG` (`T_exit` = 26,280 blocks $\approx$ 6 mo). **Seizure (`seizable = true`):** at UTXO arming, user + Fenrir co-sign a 0-fee `TRUC`/CPFP-anchored sealed seizure tx `tx_seize` under key-path `Q` paying 100% of value to immutable charter sink `D_reg`, stored threshold-encrypted under $Y_{\text{mem}}$ on Midnight and unlocked *only* upon a settled `SEIZE` `ZKAC`. No unilateral committee key leaf exists, so $n$-of-$n$ corrupt nodes still cannot divert BTC to themselves. |
| **Bitcoin (`P2WPKH`, legacy)** — `seizable = false` | Native 20-B `HASH160(X_k)` | **1 native ECDSA signature** | At UTXO arming, Fenrir delivers a co-signed `nLockTime = h_cur + T_exit` recovery sweep `tx_exit` to `D_exit` (R5). User holds a pre-armed `tx_exit` (or invokes token escape hatch after `T_exit` missed Midnight heartbeats). |
| **Ethereum (EVM) — Native ETH / plain ERC-20 (`seizable = false`)** | **Native EOA** `keccak256(X_k)[12..32]` | **1 native type-2 EOA ECDSA signature** | Each arming / nonce advance co-signs a Sealed Co-Line fallback sweep `tx_exit` (`nonce = N`, conservative `maxFeePerGas`, destination `D_exit`) valid after `T_exit` via a Midnight-free timelock relay or held offline by the user. |

The immutable vault admits **only three hardcoded transitions**: (1) `sig(X_k)` to any `ZKAC`-approved target, (2) `sig(X_seize)` to immutable `D_reg` (`SEIZE`), `D_exit` (`SWEEP`), or `held = true` (`HOLD`), and (3) `sig(K_exit_U)` to `D_exit` iff `!held && block.timestamp > lastHeartbeat + T_exit`. |

### 2.4 Recovery and exit if Fenrir disappears (R5)

Funds are never trapped under either partial or total infrastructure failure:
1. **Owner inactive, Fenrir live (dead-man standing sweep — R4(c)):** If an account misses its configured Midnight heartbeat window and has no active legal hold, any keeper triggers the Compact `StandingExit` predicate on Midnight, producing a `SWEEP_EXIT` `ZKAC` that compels the committee to sweep funds to the owner's registered `D_exit`.
2. **Fenrir and Midnight both disappear or censor forever (host-chain unilateral recovery — R5):** After `T_exit` target-chain blocks/seconds with no committee spend or heartbeat, the owner recovers directly on the host chain using **only local wallet secrets (`K_exit_U` or pre-armed `tx_exit`)**, with zero Midnight or Fenrir availability: via Taproot Leaf 1 (`<T_exit> OP_CSV <K_exit_U>`) on Bitcoin `P2TR`, via `emergencyExit(sig(K_exit_U))` on EVM/Solana seizable vaults, or by broadcasting the locally stored co-signed `tx_exit` on non-seizable EOAs / durable-nonce accounts.

### 2.5 Coinless committee rotation ($n = 9, f = 2 \to n = 17, f = 5$)

Rotating the committee from epoch $e$ (`n = 9, f = 2, t = 7`) to epoch $e + 1$ (`n' = 17, f' = 5, t' = 12`) **never touches target-chain UTXOs or accounts**, because $X_F$ and $X_{\text{seize}}$ stay invariant.

In [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L266-L270) (Finding 2 of [FINDINGS-LEDGER.md](file://design/FINDINGS-LEDGER.md)), `reshare` was restricted to $t = f + 1$ because a corrupt old dealer could send bad sub-shares to $f'$ slow honest new nodes that missed the `q'` `READY` cutoff, leaving fewer than $t' = n' - f'$ honest share-holders. `BCLD` lifts `reshare` to $t = n - f$ (`7/9` and `12/17`) without synchronous timing assumptions:
- Each old node $i \in \{1..n\}$ reshares its 5 master scalars (`[x_F]` on two curves, `[x_seize]` on two curves, `[y_mem]` on BLS12-381) plus any active armed Suite 1 presignature pairs $([w_F], [\mu_F])$ to `PK'_{e+1,1..n'}` and attaches **one succinct Plonky3-in-BLS12-381 NIZK proof $\pi_{\text{rs},i}$** ($< 2^{15}$ rows, ~80 ms once per quarterly epoch) proving that every MEGa ciphertext encrypts the exact polynomial evaluation committed in the Feldman vector `Com(g_i)` matching implied constant `Eval(C_old, i)`.
- Every board observer verifies $\pi_{\text{rs},i}$ publicly. No complaint round is needed: the first $t_{\text{old}}$ valid dealings in board order form `Q`, and **every one of the $n' - f' = t'$ honest new nodes immediately holds valid sub-shares** $v'_j = \sum_{i \in Q} \lambda_i^Q g_i(j)$. Old nodes zeroize epoch-$e$ shares once `HANDOVER(e -> e+1, Q)` commits on Midnight.

## 3. Spend flow

### 3.1 Reconciling R1 and R4(b)

We adopt the following normative reading of **R1**:

> **R1 (Charter-Gated Non-Theft & Key Separation).** *No valid target-chain signature or asset movement can be produced by any coalition of Fenrir nodes — up to all $n$ of them — without a settled Midnight Zero-Knowledge Authorization Certificate (`ZKAC`) proving one of the charter's three admitted authorization sources, and even with all $n$ nodes corrupt, non-owner paths can never route value anywhere except the immutable charter seizure sink `D_reg` or the owner's committed exit `D_exit`. The committee never holds or learns the user's share $x_U$.*

Every spend on Fenrir therefore requires **two orthogonal gates**:
1. **The Midnight `ZKAC` gate (policy & covenant enforcement):** Settled in Compact on Midnight, proving in zero knowledge that the spend satisfies every charter guardrail under authorization source `(a) PRINCIPAL_INTENT`, `(b) LEGAL_SEIZURE`, or `(c) STANDING_EXIT`.
2. **The Target-Curve Cryptographic gate (`BCLD` share combination):** Combining the initiator's cryptographic leg with $t$ committee partial signatures after BFT ordering and anchor selection.

### 3.2 Covenant predicates, named approvers, freeze, hold, and seizure on Midnight

Each asset class has an immutable `charter_hash` on Midnight defining its Compact transition predicate `CharterAllow(state, witness, pub_zkac)`. Every holder balance or registered foreign co-custody account is represented as a shielded `CovenantNote` in Midnight's Merkle tree alongside a public Dynamic Accumulator / Sparse Merkle Tree `hold_root` tracking active freezes, blacklists, and legal holds (keyed by blinded `account_tag = H(charter_hash || X_k || salt_tag)` derivable by charter-designated regulators/issuers from escrowed viewing credentials without exposing `X_k` publicly).

1. **Authorization Source (a) — Principal Intent:** The owner proves knowledge of an unspent `CovenantNote` opening for `X_k`, proves `account_tag ∉ hold_root` (a **live legal hold immediately fails this check and blocks the owner's route**, R4), proves destination compliance (either a new `CovenantNote` inheriting the **exact same `charter_hash`** across accounts/chains so the covenant follows the asset, or an external recipient holding a valid KYC/allowlist credential), and proves every named approver's non-veto (each named approver — issuer, regulator, KYT — either signs an ephemeral Compact approval credential verified inside the `ZKAC` witness or, in R5's 3-key mode, contributes sealed partial $s_{A,j}$). Even a **thief who steals the holder's wallet and $x_U$** cannot bypass velocity caps, blacklist/sanctions checks, approver vetoes, or a freeze on `account_tag`.
2. **Freeze, Blacklist, and Stolen-Fund Reversal (R4):** An authorized compliance quorum or emergency guardian settles a Compact `SetHold(account_tag, HOLD | FREEZE | BLACKLIST)` transaction on Midnight, updating `hold_root` in one Midnight block. Any in-flight Fenrir request referencing an older `hold_root` for a newly held tag is halted before Step C (`VOID_HOLD`). If theft is adjudicated under charter rules, a settled `ReverseAndReissue` transition nullifies the thief's `CovenantNote`, mints a replacement note to the recovery vault, and emits a `LEGAL_SEIZURE` `ZKAC` on the compromised foreign address.
3. **Authorization Source (b) — Legal / Default Seizure:** Admitted *only* if `charter.seizable == true`, a charter-valid court/default order threshold signature verifies inside Compact, `account_tag` is already under live `HOLD`, and all prior `PRINCIPAL_INTENT` authorizations for `X_k` have reached terminal state (`FINAL`, `VOID`, or `EXPIRED`). Emits a `ZKAC` pinned exclusively to destination `D_reg`.
4. **Authorization Source (c) — Standing Exit Sweep:** Admitted when Midnight block height exceeds `last_heartbeat + T_sweep` **and** `account_tag ∉ hold_root` (live legal hold blocks standing exit, R4). Emits a `ZKAC` pinned exclusively to `D_exit`.

**Circuit size compliance (Constraint 7):** **Committee nodes run zero ZK provers during spends.** On the initiator side (user companion app or Veritee's existing `attested-prove` service), heavy target-chain sighash hashing (`SHA-256`, `Keccak-256`, `SHA-512`) and non-native curve linear checks for the $K$ variants run inside a **Plonky3 STARK over 31-bit KoalaBear** ($2^{17}$–$2^{18}$ rows, ~0.35 s CPU), which is recursively verified once inside Midnight's **Compact / Halo2 circuit over `BLS12-381`** ($< 2^{19}$ rows $\ll 2^{21}$ limit, ~1.1 s CPU), emitting a single 1,536-byte proof $\pi_{\text{zkac}}$ (or 256-byte Midnight settlement certificate `cert_mid`) verified by committee nodes in **2.0 ms**.

### 3.3 The owner's one offline move and sealed fee-grid variants

To spend `pin` (an outpoint, an EVM nonce `N`, or a Solana durable nonce), the user's online watch-only companion fetches public epoch metadata (`epoch`, `PK_{e,1..n}`, $Y_{\text{mem}}$, `hold_root`, and for Suites 2/3 an anonymous nonce-pool slot `(pool_id, D_F, E'_F)`). The **offline signing wallet** receives this static bundle, constructs the fee grid $tx_j = \text{Variant}(\text{intent}, j)$ for $j = 1..K$ ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L247); $K = 8$ on Bitcoin, $K = 1$ on EIP-1559 EVM and Solana), computes each sighash $m_j$ (or $h_j$), and produces **one message** with no further action:

- **Sealed Co-Line Dealing (default; preserves all-$n$-corrupt user unforgeability):**
  - *Suites 2 & 3 (Schnorr):* Per variant $j$, pick fresh $k_{U,j} \xleftarrow{\$} \mathbb{Z}_q^*$, set $R_{U,j} = k_{U,j} G$, $\rho_j = Hq_{\text{BCLD/bind}}(X_k \parallel \text{pin} \parallel \text{pool\_id} \parallel R_{U,j} \parallel m_j)$, $R_j = R_{U,j} + D_F + \rho_j E'_F$ (with $\sigma_j = -1$ if Suite 2 and $y(R_j)$ is odd, else $+1$), challenge $c_j = H_{\text{sig}}(\sigma_j R_j, X_k, m_j)$, and user scalar $s_{U,j} = \sigma_j k_{U,j} + c_j (\tau x_{U,k}) \bmod q$. Deal `Share_t(s_{U,j})` in base $G$ (implied constant $F_{0,U}^{(j)} = \sigma_j R_{U,j} + c_j (\tau X_{U,k})$), plus `Share_t(\tau \cdot \text{tw}_k)` once in base $G$.
  - *Suite 1 (ECDSA):* Using armed tuple $(R_j, w_{U,j}, \mu_{U,j}, W_{U,j}, M_{U,j})$, compute $r_j = x(R_j) \bmod q$ and $s_{U,j} = h_j w_{U,j} + r_j \mu_{U,j} \bmod q$. Deal `Share_t(s_{U,j})` in base $R_j$ (implied constant $F_{0,U}^{(j)} = h_j W_{U,j} + r_j M_{U,j}$).
  - Encrypt all share evaluations to $\text{PK}_{e,1..n}$ using [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L122-L128)'s MEGa ciphertext `ct` and ephemeral-key + authorship proof $\pi$ (`z_a B = T_a + c_a F_{0,U}^{(1)}`, export-free for hardware wallets).
- **Open Co-Line Dealing (optional for dynamic multi-UTXO / DeFi `Build`):** Deals `Share_t(x_{U,k})` + two nonces `(d_U, e'_U)` (Schnorr) or `Share_t(w_{U,g}), Share_t(\mu_{U,g})` (ECDSA). Because $t$ colluding nodes could combine open user shares with committee shares to recover $x_{U,k}$ ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L317-L320) Claim 5), **Open Co-Line Dealing is permitted only on ephemeral one-shot sub-accounts (`T2`, $t = n - f$)** and never on long-lived vaults; Sealed Co-Line Dealing is mandatory for primary accounts.

The companion wraps `(OwnerMsg_BCLD, pub_zkac, \pi_zkac, Enc_{k_bcast}(tx_1..tx_K), Share_t(k_bcast))` into a fixed **16,384-byte `OrderEnvelope`** threshold-encrypted under $Y_{\text{mem}}$ (`BLS12-381` Fujisaki-Okamoto CCA2 IBE-KEM with identity `id = H("BCLD/env" || epoch || H(C_aead))`) plus an unlinkable Midnight `Zswap` fee proof $(\text{nf}_{\text{fee}}, \pi_{\text{fee}})$, and submits it once.

### 3.4 Committee execution in both modes

Both modes execute the **exact same four-phase state machine** over identical `OrderEnvelope` inputs:

1. **Step 0 — Blind Ordering (`OrderEnvelope` -> `QC_order`):** Every BFT node checks envelope size (`16,384 B`), `epoch`, unused `nf_fee`, and $\pi_{\text{fee}}$, then orders `oid = SHA-256(OrderEnvelope)`. Commit produces `QC_order(height, oid)`.
2. **Step A — Post-Order Threshold Decryption & Arming (`BOUND` -> `ARMED`):** Seeing `QC_order`, each node $i$ sends decryption share $\sigma_{\text{dec},i} = [y_{\text{mem}}]_i H_{\mathbb{G}_2}(\text{id}) \in \mathbb{G}_2$ (verifiable via $e(G_1, \sigma_{\text{dec},i}) = e(Y_{\text{mem},i}, H_{\mathbb{G}_2}(\text{id}))$) over private signing-set channels (**mutually attested `akx` sessions in TEE mode; mTLS 1.3 peer channels in non-TEE mode**). With $t$ shares, each node interpolates $\sigma_{\text{dec}}$, verifies the Fujisaki-Okamoto re-encryption check, decrypts `InnerPayload`, verifies $\pi_{\text{zkac}}$ against Midnight's finalized state and current `hold_root` (aborting with `VOID_HOLD` if `account_tag` is held), runs Step A MEGa share checks ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L151-L167)) on $F_{0,U}^{(j)}$, and posts `ACK(oid, H(mid))` (or verifiable `COMPLAINT` if MEGa fails). `q` ACKs arm `oid`.
3. **Step B — Anchor Selection (`ARMED` -> `ANCHORED`):** Identical median/quorum anchor rule `h* = entry f + 1 of q sorted OBS heights` from [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L182-L195), reading global `FeeRule(B*)` (Sealed Blinded) or `state(B*)` (TEE / Open).
4. **Step C — Co-Signing, Broadcast & Erasure (`RELEASED`/`SIGNED` -> `FINAL`):**
   - *Principal Spend (`PRINCIPAL_INTENT`):* On selected variant $j^* = \text{Select}(\text{fee\_grid}, \text{FeeRule}(B^*), j_{g-1})$ (or locked `dg` after `q` `PREVOTE`s in Open mode), node $i$ computes $s_{j^*,i} = s_{U,j^*,i} + s_{F,j^*,i}$, checks durability ledger (`one variant/digest per generation`), zeroizes generation shares, and sends `(s_{j^*,i}, [k_bcast_{j^*}]_i)` privately across the signing set. The combiner interpolates $s_{j^*}$ and $k_{\text{bcast},j^*}$, decrypts `tx_{j^*}`, verifies the single native signature against $X_k$, and broadcasts `(tx_{j^*}, sig)` to the host chain (through randomized multi-enclave `temenos-egress-proxy` relay in TEE mode; Dandelion++ P2P relayer in non-TEE mode).
   - *Seizure / Standing Sweep (`LEGAL_SEIZURE` / `STANDING_EXIT`):* Node $i$ verifies the `ZKAC` mode tag and destination hash (`D_reg` or `D_exit`) and contributes either its decryption share for pre-signed `tx_seize` (Bitcoin `P2TR`) or its threshold signature share under `[x_seize]_i` on the deterministic transfer to `D_reg` / `D_exit` (EVM/Solana).

## 4. Blindness

### 4.1 What each party sees at every step (both modes)

| Protocol phase | Public board / external observer | Node operator in **TEE mode** (controls Azure host, hypervisor, NIC, disk) | Node operator in **Non-TEE mode** (controls bare-metal process & RAM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Pending in mempool (pre-`QC_order`)** | Fixed 16 KiB `OrderEnvelope`, `oid`, anonymous Midnight `nf_fee` + $\pi_{\text{fee}}$ | Identical to public observer (cannot distinguish chains, assets, amounts, users, or seizure vs owner) | Identical to public observer (`< t` colluding operators learn 0 bits under `BLS12-381` BDH + FO-CCA2) |
| **2. Post-`QC_order`, Step A & Step B** | `QC_order(oid)`, `ACK(oid)`, `OBS(oid, g, a, chain_family, h)` — **no `X_k`, no `rid`, no `tx` on board** | Padded fixed-size inter-enclave `akx` frames (`DEC_SHARE`, `OBS`); plaintext stays inside SEV-SNP encrypted RAM | **Blinded Sealed:** sees `pub_zkac` (`nf_auth`, `hold_root`, `cm_req`), quantized fee ladder, blinded scalars `(c_j, ρ_j)` or `(h_j, r_j)`, and share `[s_{U,j}]_i` — **never owner DID, covenant terms, deposit note, `X_k`, or `tx_j`**. (*Open mode only:* sees `(X_k, intent, tx)` after `QC_order`.) |
| **3. Step C (signing, pre-host inclusion)** | `TOMBSTONE_LOCK(oid, g, H(dg))` (hash only) | Fixed-rate egress packets leaving `temenos-egress-proxy` toward target-chain RPCs | Signing set (`n` nodes) combines `s_{j^*}` and `k_bcast_{j^*}`, reconstructing `(tx_{j^*}, sig)` immediately prior to broadcast; **non-committee observers see nothing before host mempool arrival (B6)** |
| **4. Post-host inclusion (`FINAL`)** | Public host-chain `tx_{j^*}` with native single-key signature; `FINAL(oid, H(txid || salt))` on board | Cannot link `tx_{j^*}` on host chain back to specific `oid` when batched across concurrent envelopes | Knows `oid` produced `tx_{j^*}`, **still cannot** link `tx_{j^*}` to funding deposit, Midnight owner identity, covenant terms, or customer vs institution |

### 4.2 Public ordering and slashing without plaintext board leakage

In [SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L151-L242), the board published `X`, `address`, `intent`, `R_j`, and `tx` in plaintext so anyone could audit equivocation and liveness. `BCLD` keeps the board **100% auditable and slashable while content-blind**:
- **Spam & replay prevention:** $(\text{nf}_{\text{fee}}, \pi_{\text{fee}})$ burns a fixed shielded posting fee on Midnight (`Zswap`), preventing mempool flooding without linking requests. Inside `InnerPayload`, a blinded `rid_blind = H("BCLD/rid" || nf_auth)` committed inside `pub_zkac` prevents replay across envelopes.
- **Equivocation slashing:** `PREVOTE` and `LOCK` certificates on the board carry `H_dg = SHA-256(oid || g || a || B* || dg_blind)`. Signing two distinct `H_dg` values for `(oid, g)` is publicly slashable from post signatures alone without revealing `tx`.
- **Bad decryption or bad partial attribution:** Every decryption share $\sigma_{\text{dec},i} \in \mathbb{G}_2$ is publicly verifiable via pairing equation $e(G_1, \sigma_{\text{dec},i}) = e(Y_{\text{mem},i}, H_{\mathbb{G}_2}(\text{id}))$. Every blinded user/committee partial is committed against blinded curve generators $B'_j = \beta_j B_j$ embedded in `cm_req`: if optimistic combination fails on `tx_{j^*}`, a node publishes a blinded fault proof `(i, s_{j^*,i} B'_j, Eval(F'_{0}, C', i))` identifying the faulty signer $i$ on the board without disclosing `tx_{j^*}` or `X_k`.

### 4.3 Unlinkability (B5) and zero-redesign layering (B4)

- **Deposit-to-egress unlinkability:** User deposits enter Fenrir omnibus liquidity pools (or swept one-time deposit UTXOs) credited privately via Midnight `Zswap` + Compact `CovenantNote` mints. Later egress spends draw from **unlinked pool UTXOs / freshly tweaked co-custody accounts** (`X_k = X_{U,k} + X_F + \text{tw}_k G`) in quantized amount/timing batches. Chain analysts see neither a UTXO/account graph edge from deposit to egress nor any reuse of `X_F`.
- **Foreign-address & initiator indistinguishability:** Because $\text{tw}_k$ is uniform mod $q$, $(X_k, \text{sig})$ is statistically uniform among native single-key addresses on BTC, EVM, and Solana. Because `OrderEnvelope` (16 KiB) and `pub_zkac` (`(epoch, hold_root, nf_auth, cm_req, exp_mid)`) have identical byte layout and proof verification keys across `(a) PRINCIPAL_INTENT`, `(b) LEGAL_SEIZURE`, and `(c) STANDING_EXIT`, neither board observers nor non-TEE signing nodes can distinguish customer-initiated from institution-initiated egress.
- **Seamless layering (B4):** Because **TEE mode v1 already uses `OrderEnvelope`, `BLS12-381` threshold encryption under $Y_{\text{mem}}$, `ZKAC`, and `BCLD` split-line shares**, transitioning to **non-TEE mode** requires **zero changes** to user wallets, address keys, Midnight Compact contracts, or the BFT board protocol: operators simply run the exact same node binary outside SEV-SNP (replacing `akx` transport authentication with standard `Ed25519` mTLS 1.3).

### 5.2 Five gaps in Veritee today and exact additions required

Inspecting [veritee-vm](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/) reveals five concrete gaps that would break `BCLD` safety or blindness if deployed unmodified:

1. **Gap 1 — Unencrypted `/mnt/persist` disk (`AGENTS.md` warning):** [30-persist.conf](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/veritee-os/mkosi/combined.repart/30-persist.conf#L1-L7) and [mount-persist](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/veritee-os/mkosi/rootfs/mkosi.extra/usr/lib/temenos/mount-persist#L1-L38) format and mount `PARTUUID=cc428aef-829d-4387-94e8-fb81df661bd8` as **plaintext `ext4`**. Any key share or slot ledger written there is trivially read or forged via Azure disk snapshots.
   - *Addition (`temenos-sealed-persist`):* Format `persist` with `dm-crypt` (`AES-256-XTS`) stacked over `dm-integrity` (`HMAC-SHA256` authenticated sectors). Derive the master volume key via `HKDF-SHA256(k_snp_vcek || k_vtpm_nv || k_peer_lease)`, where `k_snp_vcek` comes from `/dev/sev-guest` `SNP_GET_DERIVED_KEY` (bound to `MEASUREMENT`, `HOST_DATA`, and `TCB_VERSION`), `k_vtpm_nv` is sealed in vTPM NV RAM against `PCRPolicy(PCR0..7, PCR15)`, and `k_peer_lease` is fetched in RAM at boot from $q$ committee peers (§5.2 Gap 2). **Plaintext request payloads and `OrderEnvelope` decryptions are never written to disk at all.**
2. **Gap 2 — Hypervisor VM-snapshot & vTPM rollback:** In Azure CVMs, the vTPM runs inside the HCL paravisor (`az_snp_vtpm`); an Azure host operator who snapshots **both** the VM memory/vTPM state and the virtual disk can restore an enclave to an earlier point, causing it to forget its durable `PREVOTE` / `PARTIAL` slot ledger ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L231-L237) §3.9 rule 4) and sign a second digest under one open nonce, or re-expose an erased epoch key! Also, `/var/lib/attestation_server/epoch` ([main.rs](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/server/azure_attestation_server/src/main.rs)) currently sits on the ephemeral root overlay rather than sealed storage.
   - *Addition (Nimble-style BFT Continuity Lease):* Before an enclave unwraps `[x_F]_i, [y_mem]_i` on boot — and before every `PREVOTE`/`PARTIAL` batch — it writes its monotonic state root `(boot_seq, slot_ledger_root, erased_epoch_floor)` to the Fenrir BFT board (requiring a `q`-quorum signed `LEASE_CERT(node_i, boot_seq, board_height)`). A rolled-back enclave cannot obtain `LEASE_CERT` for a stale `boot_seq`, forcing mandatory board replay up to `board_height` and re-applying all erasures before its keys unlock in RAM.
3. **Gap 3 — Key loss across OS/DApp upgrades & VM redeploys:** [EpochKey::generate](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/server/akx/src/epoch.rs#L21-L26) mints a fresh key in RAM on every boot, and changing `PCR4` (OS security patch) or `PCR15` (Fenrir node update) invalidates `SNP_GET_DERIVED_KEY` and vTPM PCR sealing.
   - *Addition (Warden-Gated Peer AKX Key Provisioning, modeled on Phala `dstack` KMS):* Long-lived master shares `[x_F]_i, [x_seize]_i, [y_mem]_i` are **never bound solely to a single VM's local disk**. When operator $i$ boots a new or upgraded CVM whose `(pcr4, pcr15, min_tcb_svn)` is approved in Midnight's `EnclaveRegistry`, the new enclave publishes its attested `GET /enclave-key` (`pk_e_new` bound in SEV-SNP `report_data`) to the committee. After verifying the VCEK report + vTPM quote against Midnight's `EnclaveRegistry` and locking `LEASE_CERT`, $t$ live peer enclaves run a zero-offset proactive share refresh directly into `pk_e_new` over `akx` — provisioning the upgraded enclave in RAM with zero operator exposure.
4. **Gap 4 — Cryptographic & RAM-level secure erasure:** Rust heap buffers in `akx` and `tokio` are not locked against paging or scrubbed deterministically on drop.
   - *Addition:* Boot kernel with `swapoff` and `crashkernel=0`; lock enclave signer heap pages via `mlockall(MCL_CURRENT | MCL_FUTURE)`; wrap all secret scalars (`sk_{e,i}`, `[y_mem]_i`, `[s_{U,j}]_i`, `[w_{F,j}]_i`) in `zeroize::Zeroizing<T>`; and wrap per-generation RAM slot tables under ephemeral `AES-256-GCM` keys ratcheted by `k_{s+1} = SHA256(k_s)` on every block so destroying one 32-byte register key cryptographically erases all completed generations.
5. **Gap 5 — Unilateral `akx` authentication and payload-length leakage:** [session.rs](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/server/akx/src/session.rs) runs `HPKE` `OpModeS::Base` (authenticating enclave to client, not peer enclave to peer enclave), and [tls-channel-binding.md](file://~/Projects/veritee/veritee-vm/docs/tls-channel-binding.md) notes (`A11`) that `POST /proxy` reveals record byte lengths to the host NIC.
   - *Addition (`akx-mutual-padded`):* Extend `SessionCompleteRequest` with optional peer `client_epoch_pub` + `client_attestation` + transcript signature `sig_c` verified against Midnight's `EnclaveRegistry`, pad all inter-enclave `akx` records to fixed `4,096 B` cells, and emit cover heartbeats at a constant tick rate (10 Hz) between signing peers.

### 5.3 What a malicious Azure host operator can still do in TEE mode

An operator with full root control over the Azure host / hypervisor **cannot** read SEV-SNP guest RAM (`RMP` hardware enforcement), forge VCEK attestations, decrypt `OrderEnvelope`s, inspect `tx` before host broadcast, or replay old disk images past the BFT `LEASE_CERT` check. The operator **can** still:
1. **Halt, reboot, or drop packets (liveness DoS):** Kill the VM, block NIC traffic, or delay `akx` frames. With $\le f$ such operators ($2$ of $9$, $5$ of $17$), the remaining $n - f \ge t$ enclaves preserve full liveness without them.
2. **Observe coarse-grained resource & timing side channels:** See total CPU load spikes and NIC packet counts (mitigated by constant-rate padded 4 KiB peer `akx` cells and batch envelope decryption).
3. **Mount physical / microarchitectural attacks on AMD SEV-SNP (`Ciphertext-sidechannel`, `CacheWarp`, voltage fault injection, or AMD supply-chain compromise):** We treat TEE hardware compromise explicitly in §6.3 — **because `BCLD` runs threshold encryption and sealed user co-dealing inside the enclaves**, breaking $< t$ TEEs compromises neither pending blindness nor any key, and breaking **all $n$ TEEs** degrades gracefully to the non-TEE security floor rather than catastrophic key theft!

## 6. Security claims

### 6.1 Ideal functionality `F_BCLD`

`F_BCLD` extends `F_OMS` ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L278-L293)) with Midnight ledger `M` (`EnclaveRegistry`, `charter_hash`, `hold_root`), master keys $(X_F, X_{\text{seize}}, Y_{\text{mem}})$, and blind submission:
- **SetupAddress:** Creates $X_k = X_{U,k} + X_F + \text{tw}_k G$, storing $x_{U,k}$ at User and recording `cm_acct` on `M`, leaking nothing to Adversary $\mathcal{A}$.
- **SubmitBlind:** On `(submit, env_input)` from an initiator with a valid `M`-admitted witness under source `src ∈ {PRINCIPAL_INTENT, LEGAL_SEIZURE, STANDING_EXIT}`, leaks only `(oid, bucket_size = 16 KiB)` before `QC_order`.
- **Order & Execute:** Upon `QC_order(oid)` and an anchored eligible target state $B^*$, verifies `account_tag ∉ hold_root` (for owner/sweep routes), selects variant $j^*$ (or builds `tx` in Open mode), and outputs `(tx_{j^*}, Sign(x_k, tx_{j^*}))` (or forced-transfer signature to `D_reg` / `D_exit`). In TEE mode and Blinded Sealed non-TEE mode, leaks `(tx_{j^*}, sig)` to $\mathcal{A}$ only upon Step C broadcast readiness.

### 6.2 Precise claims and proof sketches (both modes)

**Claim 1 (Charter-Gated Non-Theft & User Key Secrecy — R1, R4).**
*Adversary:* Static corruption of **up to all $n$ committee nodes** ($|C| \le n$), with **all TEEs broken** (equivalent to non-TEE mode), honest user wallet, and sound Midnight `ZKAC` verifier.
*Statement:* $\mathcal{A}$ learns zero information about $x_{U,k}$ from any polynomial number of Sealed Co-Line requests and cannot output a valid target-chain transaction moving funds from $X_k$ outside `{Variant(intent, 1..K)}` (from user requests) $\cup$ `{D_reg, D_exit}` (when `seizable = true` or exit sweep is pre-armed).
*Reduction sketch:*
- *Suites 2 & 3 (Schnorr):* Reduction $\mathcal{R}$ receives challenge public key $X_U^*$ from the standard Schnorr/BIP-340/Ed25519 `EUF-CMA` challenger, picks $x_F, \text{tw}_k$ itself (controlling all $n$ corrupt nodes!), sets $X_k = X_U^* + (x_F + \text{tw}_k)G$, and for each Sealed Co-Line variant $j$ queries its `EUF-CMA` signing oracle under $X_U^*$ with programmed random-oracle challenge $c_j = H_{\text{sig}}(\sigma_j R_j, X_k, m_j)$ (programming $H_{\text{sig}}$ after getting $R_{U,j}$ from the oracle and applying $\rho_j = Hq_{\text{bind}}(X_k \parallel \text{pin} \parallel R_{U,j} \parallel m_j)$) to obtain $s_{U,j}$, then deals `Share_t(s_{U,j})` honestly. Any signature $(R^*, s^*)$ produced by $\mathcal{A}$ under $X_k$ on $m^* \notin \{m_1..m_K\}$ yields a Schnorr forgery under $X_U^*$ after subtracting $(x_F + \text{tw}_k) c^*$ (or breaks Taproot `OP_CSV` key $K_{\text{exit},U}$).
- *Suite 1 (ECDSA):* Reduction $\mathcal{R}$ reduces to `[GS22]` (`ePrint 2021/1330` Theorem 3) single-signer presignature unforgeability in the EC-GGM + ROM and `DKLs23` OT-OLE simulation. During 1-round setup, $\mathcal{R}$ extracts $\mathcal{A}$'s $(k_{F,j}, w_{F,j}, \mu_{F,j})$ via the OT simulator and embeds the `[GS22]` challenger's nonce point $R_j$. On spend, $\mathcal{R}$ queries the `[GS22]` signing oracle on `(j, h_j)` once to get $s_j$, sets $s_{U,j} = s_j - (h_j w_{F,j} + r_j \mu_{F,j}) \bmod q$, and deals `Share_t(s_{U,j})` (whose base-$R_j$ constant term equals $s_j R_j - (h_j W_{F,j} + r_j M_{F,j}) = h_j W_{U,j} + r_j M_{U,j}$ identically). A forgery on $h^* \notin \{h_1..h_K\}$ wins `[GS22]` Theorem 3 directly. On `seizable = true` vaults/Taproot, `D_reg` and `D_exit` are immutable on-chain constants, so `[x_seize]` cannot divert funds elsewhere.

**Claim 2 (Mandatory Network Participation & Thief Containment — R2, R4).**
*Adversary:* Malicious user (or wallet thief holding $x_{U,k}$ and `K_exit_U`) colluding with **$|C| < t$ corrupt committee nodes** ($|C| \le 6$ at launch, $|C| \le 11$ at scale; $b \le f$ active Byzantine), in **both TEE and non-TEE modes** while Fenrir is live ($< T_{\text{exit}}$).
*Statement:* $\mathcal{A}$ cannot produce a valid spend on $X_k$ without a valid settled Midnight `ZKAC` verified by at least $t - |C| \ge 1$ honest committee nodes (so live holds, freezes, blacklists, velocity limits, and approver vetoes are unbypassable).
*Reduction sketch:* Honest committee nodes release partial signatures $s_{F,j,i}$ only after verifying $\pi_{\text{zkac}}$ and `account_tag ∉ hold_root` in Step A and `Eligible` in Step B. With $|C| < t$, `[x_F]` has $t - |C| \ge 1$ missing Shamir degrees of freedom. For Schnorr, committee two-nonce shares $([d_F], [e'_F])$ bound by $\rho_j = Hq_{\text{bind}}(X_k \parallel \text{pin} \parallel R_{U,j} \parallel m_j)$ reduce forgery by $\mathcal{A}$ (even choosing $X_{U,k}$ and $R_{U,k}$ maliciously!) to `AOMDL` ([PROOFS.md](file://design/PROOFS.md) Theorem B / `ROAST` `ePrint 2022/550`). For ECDSA, `[w_{F,j}], [\mu_{F,j}]` reduce to `[GS22]` Theorem 3 under $X_F$. If `account_tag` enters `hold_root`, Fenrir sweeps/locks the UTXO or sets `vault.held = true` well before `T_exit` expires, blocking the thief's `K_exit_U` timeout path.

**Claim 3 (Pre-Ordering Mempool Confidentiality — B2, B3).**
*Adversary:* Static corruption of **$|C| < t$ committee nodes** (and arbitrary host operators in TEE mode; up to $t - 1$ broken TEEs in TEE mode, or $t - 1$ corrupt bare-metal operators in non-TEE mode).
*Statement:* Before `QC_order(oid)` is committed on the BFT board, `OrderEnvelope` is `IND-CCA2` indistinguishable from an encryption of $16\text{ KiB}$ of zeros, and `nf_fee` is `ZK`-unlinkable to any Midnight account or `X_k`.
*Reduction sketch:* Honest nodes never compute or emit $\sigma_{\text{dec},i} = [y_{\text{mem}}]_i H_{\mathbb{G}_2}(\text{id})$ until verifying `QC_order(oid)` (`2q - n \ge f + 1` honest quorum intersection ensures `oid` is irrevocably fixed in board order). With $|C| < t$ shares of $y_{\text{mem}}$, `IND-CCA2` reduces tightly to Bilinear Diffie-Hellman (`BDH`) on `BLS12-381` in the ROM via the Fujisaki-Okamoto transform (Boneh-Franklin / `vetKeys` `ePrint 2023/616`), where the simulator answers post-order decryption queries on `id' ≠ id*` by programming $H_{\mathbb{G}_2}(\text{id}') = a_{\text{id}'} G_2$ and setting $\sigma_{\text{dec}}(\text{id}') = a_{\text{id}'} \psi(Y_{\text{mem}})$ without knowing $y_{\text{mem}}$.

**Claim 4 (Post-Order Operator Blindness & Unlinkability — B1, B5, B6).**
*Adversary:*
- *TEE mode:* **All $n$ host operators** passive/malicious on the Azure host, provided SEV-SNP + vTPM hardware confidentiality holds on the signing enclaves.
- *Non-TEE mode:* **$|C| < t$ corrupt operators** in Blinded Sealed mode (and all $n$ operators with respect to owner identity, covenant terms, deposit origin, and customer-vs-institution origin).
*Statement:* Operators learn zero bits about owner identity, Midnight deposit note, covenant predicates, or customer-vs-institution authorization source (`ZK` property of Halo2/Compact `ZKAC` + `Zswap`). Furthermore, in TEE mode (all spends) and non-TEE Blinded Sealed mode ($|C| < t$), operators learn neither `X_k` nor `tx_{j^*}` prior to Step C broadcast reconstruction, and external observers outside the $n$-node signing set never see `tx_{j^*}` prior to host-chain broadcast (B6).
*Reduction sketch:* In TEE mode, `InnerPayload` is decrypted exclusively inside SEV-SNP guest pages protected by AMD hardware `RMP` and transmitted only over `akx-mutual-padded` `AES-256-GCM` channels. In non-TEE Blinded Sealed mode, `tx_1..tx_K` are encrypted under `Share_t(k_bcast)` (protected by `ICDH` MEGa encryption, [PROOFS.md](file://design/PROOFS.md) Theorem C, until $t$ nodes release Step C shares), while $\text{tw}_k$ masks $X_k = X_{U,k} + X_F + \text{tw}_k G$ uniformly on the curve.

**Claim 5 (Liveness & Coinless Rotation Safety — R5, Constraint 5).**
*Adversary:* $b \le f$ Byzantine committee nodes under partial synchrony.
*Statement:* Every eligible charter-compliant request finalizes within 1 board commit + 2 P2P rounds (Sealed) or 3 P2P rounds (Open) after GST, and epoch rotation `ROTATE(e -> e+1)` preserves `one digest per slot`, master key secrecy, and full $t'$-threshold signing availability with zero target-chain transactions.
*Proof sketch:* Honest nodes number $n - f = t \ge q$, so they alone form `QC_order`, the decryption share threshold $t$, the `ACK` and `OBS` quorums $q$, and the partial signature threshold $t$. At rotation, soundness of $\pi_{\text{rs},i}$ guarantees every accepted dealing in `Q` (`|Q| = t_old`) encrypts valid shares to all $n'$ new nodes, so all $n' - f' = t'$ honest new nodes immediately hold valid recombined shares $v'_j$.

### 6.3 Explicit failure analysis when TEE hardware is broken in TEE mode

Because `BCLD` layers threshold cryptography *inside* the Veritee enclaves from day one rather than trusting a single enclave with whole keys:
- **If $1 \le k < t$ TEEs are completely broken (e.g., side-channel or physical lab extraction on up to 6 of 9 launch enclaves):**
  - *What holds:* **R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, B2, B3, and B5 all remain 100% intact!** Fewer than $t$ broken enclaves cannot decrypt pending `OrderEnvelope`s before `QC_order` (B3), cannot reconstruct $x_F$, $x_{\text{seize}}$, or $y_{\text{mem}}$, and cannot forge or bypass covenants.
  - *What fails:* **B1 (for `tx` and `X_k` post-ordering) and B6 degrade to the non-TEE bound** on the broken nodes: in Open mode, a broken enclave's operator sees `(X_k, tx)` after `QC_order` ~200 ms before host broadcast (owner identity, deposit link, and covenant terms *still* stay hidden behind Midnight's `ZKAC`!).
- **If $\ge t$ (up to all $n$) TEEs are broken AND $\ge t$ operators actively collude:**
  - *What STILL holds:* **R1 and Sealed user unforgeability still hold!** Colluding operators with broken TEEs still **never learn $x_{U,k}$** and cannot forge any spend on $X_k$ outside the user's sealed fee variants `{tx_1..tx_K}` (or immutable `D_reg` / `D_exit`), nor can they strip owner identity or deposit history from Midnight's `Zswap` / `ZKAC` proofs!
  - *What fails:* Colluding $\ge t$ broken-TEE operators can decrypt `OrderEnvelope`s before `QC_order` (breaking B2/B3), see `tx` before broadcast (breaking B1/B6), sign without checking `ZKAC` on `X_F` *only if* a stolen-wallet thief also supplies $x_{U,k}$ (breaking R4 when combined with wallet theft), or trigger an un-authorized sweep strictly to `D_reg` / `D_exit` on `seizable = true` accounts.

### 8.2 What breaks in `SCHEME.md`

1. **"No DKG, no presignature protocol, no OT" ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L5)):** Replaced. Two-key co-custody (R2, R3) requires a master committee DKG ($x_F, x_{\text{seize}}, y_{\text{mem}}$), an epoch two-nonce pool for Schnorr, and a 1-round `DKLs23` OT-OLE setup at address arming for active Suite 1 ECDSA accounts.
2. **Importing an arbitrary pre-existing single-key address without moving coins:** Dropped. Under R2/R3, an address whose full private key $x$ was already known to the user wallet violates *"no single party holds the private key of the controlling address"*; existing assets enter Fenrir by depositing to a network-created `BCLD` address or omnibus pool.
3. **Plaintext public board auditability of `X`, `intent`, and `tx` ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L151-L164)):** Replaced. Public nodes before `QC_order` see only 16 KiB `OrderEnvelope`s and `Zswap` fee proofs; `X` and `tx` checks move into Midnight's `ZKAC` + post-`QC_order` private signing-set verification.
4. **`reshare` forbidden at $t = n - f$ ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L38)):** Fixed by attaching a small-field STARK-in-BLS12-381 proof $\pi_{\text{rs}}$ once per epoch rotation.
5. **Raw clamped Ed25519 scalar dealing ([SCHEME.md](file://design/SCHEME.md#L147)):** Replaced by additive mod-$\ell$ scalar derivation (`clamp(SHA-512(seed)) mod ℓ`) for both $x_{U,k}$ and $[x_F^{\text{ed}}]$.

### 8.3 New assumptions

- **Cryptographic (both modes):** Bilinear Diffie-Hellman (`BDH`) and `q-SDH` / Knowledge-of-Exponent on `BLS12-381` (for threshold FO-CCA2 `OrderEnvelope` KEM, Midnight Halo2/Compact `ZKAC` soundness, and `Zswap` fee privacy); collision resistance of `Poseidon2` / `BLAKE3` inside the Plonky3 KoalaBear STARK ($< 2^{18}$ rows); and malicious-security of 1-round `DKLs23` OT-OLE (`KOS`/`SoftSpokenOT` in ROM) for Suite 1 arming.
- **Hardware & platform (TEE mode only, for B1 Open-mode blindness and defense-in-depth):** Confidentiality and `RMP` integrity of AMD SEV-SNP guest memory at the `EnclaveRegistry`-pinned `min_tcb_svn`, unforgeability of AMD VCEK keys and Azure HCL `az_snp_vtpm` PCR quotes, and reproducibility of Warden `dm-verity` measurements on Midnight.

8. **§3.9–3.10 (Local rules & Finish):** Add Veritee TEE continuity rule (`LEASE_CERT` quorum check before unsealing keys or voting) and RAM-only `Zeroizing` + ratcheted `AES-256-GCM` slot erasure; replace plaintext `FINAL(rid, txid)` with blinded `FINAL(oid, H(txid || salt))`.
