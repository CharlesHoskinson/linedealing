### Blocking findings

1. **Unbounded liveness deadlock and adversarial block-selection option in Step B (`OBS`) under partial synchrony (Section 3.3 Step B; Section 6.8 Claim 7).**
   - **Flaw:** Step B defines the anchor block `B*` as *"the first block that collects `f + 1` OBS posts in board order"*, where each node posts a single `OBS(rid, g, B)` upon seeing a finalized target-chain block `B` in which `trigger` holds and `pin` is unspent (confirmed by Section 7's budget of 4 posts total per node per request). Target chains produce an ongoing sequence of finalized blocks, and under the stated partial-synchrony model (Section 2), honest nodes observe different latest finalized blocks when `nb` or `trigger` becomes active. Claim 7 asserts that `2f + 1` honest nodes posting `OBS` guarantees completion within a constant number of board rounds after GST.
   - **Concrete attack / counterexample:** Let `n = 16, f = 5` (`2f + 1 = 11` honest nodes, required `OBS` threshold `f + 1 = 6`). Before GST (or across consecutive blocks on a fast-finality chain), stagger target-chain block delivery across three finalized blocks `B_1, B_2, B_3` so that `5` honest nodes see `B_1` and post `OBS(rid, g, B_1)`, `5` honest nodes see `B_2` and post `OBS(rid, g, B_2)`, and `1` honest node sees `B_3` and posts `OBS(rid, g, B_3)`. Every block has at most `5 < 6` posts (in general `(f, f, 1)` across `2f + 1` honest nodes, each `< f + 1`). If the `5` Byzantine nodes stay silent, `B*` is never defined and the request deadlocks forever, falsifying Claim 7. Worse, the `f` Byzantine nodes gain a free option up to `exp` to unilaterally pick either `state(B_1)` or `state(B_2)` by posting one `OBS` at a time of their choosing, or withhold it to abort.
   - **Required fix:** Replace uncoordinated single-shot `OBS` exact-block tallying with either (a) BFT leader proposal of an eligible finalized block `B` (certified by `2f + 1` votes after honest nodes catch up to `B`, with standard view-change retry), or (b) monotonic height voting where `B*` is the canonical finalized block at the `(f + 1)`-st highest finalized height reported among `2f + 1` `OBS` posts.

2. **Permanent share-starvation deadlock during `reshare` under `t = 2f + 1` without verifiable encryption (Section 3.3 Step A; Section 5 `reshare`).**
   - **Flaw:** `cand-3c91` sets reconstruction threshold `t' = 2f + 1` (`n = 3f + 1`) and uses hashed ElGamal without NIZK verifiable encryption, relying on *"ACK quorum (`2f + 1` ACKs) of the new committee, no valid complaint"* before committing the handover certificate, after which *"Old nodes erase after the handover certificate is on the board"*. In partial synchrony, `2f + 1` ACKs certify only `(2f + 1) - f = f + 1` honest shares—leaving a gap of `f` shares below `t' = 2f + 1` while `f` delayed honest new nodes have not yet been able to post complaints.
   - **Concrete attack:** At rotation from `C_old` to `C_new` (`n = 16, f = 5, t' = 11`), **1 Byzantine old node** `i = 1` deals polynomial `g_1` (`g_1(0) = v_1`) with valid ciphertexts to the `5` Byzantine nodes in `C_new` and `6` honest nodes in `C_new`, and encrypts random garbage scalars to the remaining `5` honest nodes in `C_new`. All honest old nodes then erase `v_i` and `sk_i`. After GST, the `5` delayed honest new nodes cannot decrypt valid shares (and old shares are gone), while the `5` Byzantine new nodes go silent in Step C.    - **Required fix:** Either lower the threshold to `t = f + 1` (sacrificing the additional `f` passive-leakage cushion so `2f + 1` ACKs guarantee `f + 1 = t` honest shares), or attach a publicly checkable NIZK proof of correct share encryption (e.g., `cgVSS` or chunked ElGamal PVSS) during `reshare` so ciphertext validity does not depend on absence of delayed complaints.

   - **Required fix:** Commit an atomic `FREEZE(rid)` entry on the board before old nodes emit resharings; reject any old-committee `PREVOTE`/`PARTIAL` for generation `g` ordered after `FREEZE(rid)` unless `g` already achieved a full `2f + 1` matching `PREVOTE` certificate before `FREEZE(rid)`; reshare every generation lacking such a pre-freeze `2f + 1` certificate.

Dealing the **linear signature equation** rather than raw key shares—specifically, having the owner share `(w = k⁻¹, μ = k⁻¹x)` with Feldman commitments in base `R = kG` whose implied constant terms are publicly forced to `w·R = G` and `μ·R = X` in Open mode, alongside sharing pre-evaluated signature scalars `s` committed against `F_0 = hG + rX` (base `R`) or `F_0 = R + cX` (base `G`) across a pin-conflicting fee grid in Sealed mode—is a major design breakthrough for this brief. ## Head-to-head

**cand-3c91** is the better base design. Its central algebraic idea—dealing the linear signature equation (`w = k⁻¹`, `μ = k⁻¹x` committed in base `R = kG` with implied constant terms `G` and `X`, or sealed `s` scalars over a pin-conflicting fee grid) rather than raw key shares—solves the brief's hardest cryptographic problems without heavy machinery. Whereas **cand-5d08** covers only Taproot (leaving ECDSA and Gap 2 completely open), relies on complex class-group assumptions (exposed to the `Cl(Δ_q)` order-2 form soundness bug and `34.8 MB` migration payloads), and locks one un-bumpable fee, **cand-3c91** natively covers existing ECDSA, BIP340/Taproot, and Ed25519 addresses using only standard curve arithmetic, zero committee preprocessing, 4–5× smaller owner packets, and a sealed mode giving cryptographic single-spend security even against `n` corrupt nodes and hardware-only wallets. Every blocking flaw in **cand-3c91** sits in its coordination layer (`OBS` checkpoint selection, un-frozen `reshare` races, `t = 2f + 1` resharing without verifiable encryption, and key-domain separation) and is cleanly fixed by adopting **cand-5d08**'s BFT `LOCK`/freeze state machine and `t = f + 1` threshold.
