Latency, simulated

Forty real requests per committee size, per-node work measured in thread CPU time, placed on a discrete-event schedule. Fixed C: a commit lands C after submission (the DESIGN §11.2 model). Random-phase blocks: a submission waits for the next block start, then C. Each peer round delivers h after sending. Witness and the two bind rounds are pure delay. Midnight-first rows give the time after FM; board-first rows exclude the proof check, which the prototype does not have. h = 50 ms rows shown; the h = 25 and 0 rows are in bench-results/split-latency.txt.
